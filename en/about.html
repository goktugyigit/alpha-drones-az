Who We Are

Full-Service Drone Company

Founded in 2025 by Hasan Aliyev and Maximilian Fraydenegg, Alpha Drones is a full-service drone-focused technology company. Our core mission is to lead the evolution of drone solutions in cleaning, inspection, mapping, and agriculture — making urban and natural environments safer, smarter, and more sustainable. Our vision is to position Azerbaijan as a regional leader in drone technology and innovation. Guided by the principle "Safe, Smart, Green," we bring the technology of tomorrow to industrial processes today.
