Indoor Drone Inspection

Safe Inspections in Hazardous Spaces

Alpha Drones specializes in confined space inspection using collision-tolerant indoor drones designed to operate in the most challenging environments. Our drones navigate through tanks, silos, boilers, tunnels, storage vessels, and other enclosed industrial spaces that pose significant risks to human inspectors including toxic atmospheres, extreme temperatures, and fall hazards.

  • No human entry needed — eliminate exposure to toxic gases and extreme heat
  • Collision-tolerant drones with protective cage frames for tight spaces
  • HD video capture with powerful LED lighting in dark environments
  • Full safety compliance with confined space entry regulations
Advanced Technology

Detailed Visual Data Without Risk

Equipped with high-definition cameras, powerful LED lighting, and protective cage frames, our confined space drones capture detailed visual data of internal surfaces, welds, corrosion, and structural integrity without anyone needing to enter the space. This eliminates the need for scaffolding inside vessels, reduces downtime, and ensures full compliance with health and safety regulations.

  • 4K HD cameras for crystal-clear inspection footage
  • Detects corrosion, cracks, and weld defects accurately
  • No scaffolding required inside vessels — reduces downtime
  • Contact recovery capability for safe navigation in tight areas
