Aerial Surveillance

Eyes in the Sky, Around the Clock

Alpha Drones provides advanced aerial patrolling services using drones equipped with both high-resolution visual and radiometric thermal cameras. Our patrolling solutions deliver real-time situational awareness for security operations, energy infrastructure monitoring, oil and gas pipeline inspection, and perimeter surveillance of critical facilities.

  • Live HD video and thermal feeds streamed to your operations center
  • Detect gas leaks, electrical faults, and overheating equipment
  • Day and night operations with full thermal imaging capability
  • Patrol kilometers of pipeline or perimeter in a single flight
Intelligent Detection

Thermal Intelligence Beyond Human Sight

With the ability to detect thermal anomalies such as gas leaks, electrical hotspots, and unauthorized human activity, our drones provide a layer of intelligence that ground-based systems simply cannot match. Automated flight paths ensure consistent coverage, while live video feeds and alerts keep your operations center informed at all times.

  • Radiometric thermal imaging for precise temperature measurement
  • Automated GPS waypoint missions for consistent coverage
  • Real-time alerts for unauthorized activity or anomalies
  • Ideal for oil & gas, energy, and critical infrastructure
