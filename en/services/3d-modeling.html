Digital Twin Technology

Your Assets in Digital Form

Alpha Drones uses advanced photogrammetry and LiDAR technology to create highly accurate 3D digital twins of buildings, infrastructure, terrain, and industrial assets. These digital replicas serve as living models that can be used for design validation, construction progress tracking, facility management, and long-term asset monitoring.

  • Millimeter-level photogrammetric precision from aerial photographs
  • BIM-compatible exports for Revit, AutoCAD, and SketchUp
  • Real-time construction progress tracking with 3D captures
  • Accurate as-built documentation for facility management
BIM & CAD Integration

Seamless Data-Driven Workflows

Our 3D models integrate seamlessly with BIM platforms and CAD software, enabling architects, engineers, and project managers to make data-driven decisions. From as-built documentation of completed structures to regular progress captures during construction, our digital twin service provides unmatched visibility into your projects.

  • Direct export to OBJ, FBX, IFC, and LAS formats
  • High-density point clouds up to 1000 pts/m²
  • Design validation against original plans and blueprints
  • Long-term asset monitoring and change detection
